# Quiz Arena age rating and content rights review

This document prepares the remaining App Store information for version 1.0.0.

## Age rating

Apple requires every App Store app to complete the current age rating questionnaire.

Do not manually assume a final age rating before completing the questionnaire in App Store Connect.

The questionnaire result can vary by country or region.

## Quiz Arena content currently relevant to the questionnaire

1. Quiz Arena is primarily an educational quiz app.
2. The app does not include user generated content.
3. The app does not include messaging or chat.
4. The app does not include social networking features.
5. The app does not include gambling or simulated gambling.
6. The app does not include contests or chance based rewards involving real money.
7. The app does not include advertising in the current release.
8. The app does not include unrestricted web browsing.
9. The app includes historical educational questions about the First World War and Second World War.
10. Historical questions can refer to battles, deaths, persecution, war crimes and other serious historical events.
11. These references are intended as factual educational text, not graphic visual violence.
12. No graphic war imagery is intended in the current Quiz Arena interface.
13. The Islam section is intended as educational religious content.
14. Mathematics, Geography, Science, EU and general knowledge sections are educational quiz content.

## Questionnaire review points

When the App Store Connect age rating questionnaire is opened, verify every answer against the release candidate.

Pay particular attention to:

1. Violence.
2. Mature or suggestive themes.
3. Horror or fear themes.
4. Medical or wellness content.
5. Gambling or chance based activities.
6. User generated content.
7. Messaging or chat.
8. Advertising.
9. Social media capabilities.
10. Unrestricted web access.

For historical war content, select the frequency and severity that accurately describes the final question set.

Do not classify factual references to war as graphic violence unless the release candidate actually contains graphic descriptions or images.

## Made for Kids

Do not select Made for Kids for version 1.0.0 unless there is a deliberate product decision to enter Apple's Kids category.

Once an approved app is marked Made for Kids, later versions must continue to comply with the Kids category requirements.

Quiz Arena can still be suitable for younger users without selecting the Kids category.

## Content Rights

Apple requires apps that contain, display or access third party content to have the necessary rights or legal permission for that content in every country or region where the app is distributed.

Before answering the Content Rights question, verify the following.

1. Quiz Arena owns or has permission to use all app branding, icons and splash artwork.
2. Quiz Arena owns or has permission to use every image, illustration, audio file and other media included in the app.
3. Quiz question wording is original, licensed or otherwise legally permitted.
4. Source links are references and do not imply ownership of third party material.
5. The app does not copy substantial protected text from external websites or books.
6. Any quoted material remains within permitted use and is properly attributed where required.
7. External source websites are opened as links rather than embedded or republished as owned content.
8. No third party logo, photograph, illustration or copyrighted asset is included without permission.

## Current Quiz Arena asset observation

The main Quiz Arena V2 screen uses application code, CSS, text content and the project's own interface assets.

No separate third party media import was identified directly in the QuizArenaLiveAppV2 screen during this review.

This does not replace a final repository and signed build asset review.

## Final Content Rights decision

Do not answer the Content Rights field solely from this checklist.

Before submission, inspect the exact release candidate and confirm that every bundled asset and question source is permitted.

If the app only uses original Quiz Arena content plus lawful factual references and external source links, record that basis internally before submission.

## Release gate

Age Rating and Content Rights are complete only when:

1. The final App Store Connect age rating questionnaire has been answered against the release candidate.
2. The resulting regional ratings have been reviewed.
3. The Content Rights answer matches the actual app content.
4. All bundled media and question content have been checked for permission or lawful use.
5. No unresolved copyright or licensing concern remains.
